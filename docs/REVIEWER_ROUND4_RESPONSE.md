## New aggregate evidence

The existing auditable two-part decomposition reports an extensive-margin coefficient of 0.004659 (firm-clustered SE 0.007521, p=0.5358, estimator N=6,443) and an intensive-margin coefficient of 0.065300 (SE 0.050626, p=0.1980, estimator N=1,093). These are descriptive components, not a structural hurdle or ZIP model. They show no detectable extensive-margin association and an imprecise positive-count intensive-margin association under their distinct target populations.
