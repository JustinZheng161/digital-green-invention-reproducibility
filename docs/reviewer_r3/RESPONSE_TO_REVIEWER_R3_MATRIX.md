# R3 Revision Matrix: Reviewer Requests, Evidence, and Action

## Editorial position

This revision uses the more stringent R3 report (`pasted_content_6.txt`) as the **minimum evidentiary threshold**, while incorporating the clarity requests in `pasted_content_5.txt`. The study is positioned as a transparent, observational analysis of a deterministic match between two public source files. It does not claim a causal effect, a population-average treatment effect, or a general measure of green-innovation quality.

The revision follows three reporting principles synthesized from Nature Portfolio, Science, and *Scientific Data* guidance: define the analytic population separately for every estimator; report effect estimates with sample sizes, intervals, exclusions, and multiple-testing scope; and distinguish released data fields, analyst transformations, and unavailable source information.

## Locked interpretation for R3

> The available matched sources support a reproducible descriptive association analysis. The log-outcome and conditional-count results are statistically imprecise at the conventional 5% level in their respective samples, and the conditional PPML result pertains only to the estimator-retained contribution set. Lagged, source-labelled proxy sensitivities do not remove the uncertainty or identify a causal effect. A study designed to support a substantive causal claim would require a pre-specified design and a harmonized, licensed source that verifies R&D intensity, patent stock, field construction, and sample inclusion.
