## Statistical-model rationale

PPML remains the preferred count specification because it accommodates the nonnegative count outcome and its zero mass while using firm and year fixed effects [1] [2]. We did **not** claim that a large fraction of zeros alone proves a zero-inflated or hurdle process: ZIP and hurdle models rely on different assumptions about the generating process for zeros, which cannot be verified from the released source fields [3]. We therefore report a two-part **descriptive** decomposition to clarify occurrence and conditional intensity, not a structural hurdle/ZIP causal model [4].

## Reproducibility and access

The revised analysis is executed by the locked scripts in the public repository and reports only aggregate output. Raw archives, extracted fields and the matched firm-year panel are retained in the private companion repository because the underlying CNRDS, CSMAR, Wind and annual-report source terms may constrain redistribution. Both original archives were SHA-256 verified. The updated public release adds the R1 analysis script, aggregate diagnostic tables/figures and this response document; no row-level identifiers or raw data are pushed publicly.
