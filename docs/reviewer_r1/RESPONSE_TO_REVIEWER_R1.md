## Analysis conventions locked before rerun

The primary family for multiple-testing adjustment is **S1–S5**: five two-way-fixed-effect linear models sharing the same log collaborative-green-invention outcome, with alternative DT transformations and declared control sets. M3 PPML, M4 utility-patent model, timing tests and two-part decompositions answer different outcome/design questions and will not be pooled into that specific family. This protects against both under-correction and a meaningless one-family-for-everything adjustment.

The revised study will not perform or claim a fixed-effects ZIP/Hurdle causal model. Such models require additional distributional/identification assumptions and are not necessary to meet the requested correction. Instead, it will provide a transparent two-part descriptive decomposition and present PPML as the preferred count model, consistent with the available data and fixed-effects design.
